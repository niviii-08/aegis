AEGIS: A Resilient Multimodal Framework for Cross-Domain Deepfake and Synthetic Media Detection

Neevetha N.
AEGIS Research Project • October 2026
Repository: github.com/niviii-08/aegis
Abstract: The exponential proliferation of generative artificial intelligence has dramatically democratized the synthesis of hyper-realistic audio, image, and video content. We present AEGIS, an end-to-end multimodal deepfake detection system engineered for robust cross-domain generalization. AEGIS unifies three specialized forensic pipelines: (1) an Audio Forensic Engine (MelCNN) trained across ASVspoof 2019, FoR, WaveFake (10 vocoders), and ASVspoof 2021 (lossy codecs); (2) a Visual Forensic Engine (EfficientNet-B4) enhanced with Laplacian frequency variance heuristics; and (3) a Temporal Video Engine (CNN-BiLSTM). Empirical benchmarks demonstrate 100.0% ROC-AUC on WaveFake vocoders, 84.97% ROC-AUC on compressed ASVspoof 2021 attacks, and 89.76% accuracy on facial deepfakes.
